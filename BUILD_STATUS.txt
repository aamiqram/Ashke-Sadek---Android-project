Voice-text matching + honest mic feedback (Nov 9, 2026 working tree, awaiting push):

- Fuzzy n-gram fallback for voice suggestions: when strict word-order evidence
  disappears (sung audio), character n-gram containment of the transcript tail
  against every song's normalized lyric text keeps the real song suggested.
  Sketch sampling (256 hashes/song, ~1.2 MB) keeps the per-refresh cost tiny;
  verification is exact. Constants verified by tests/calibrate_voice_fuzzy.py
  over the real catalogue: ~96-98% recall for lightly garbled sung transcripts
  in the top suggestions, ~35% for heavily garbled ones, ~0.13 stray
  conversation candidates per chatter window. CI runs this calibration on
  every build.
- Microphone audio hint: when sustained loud sound is heard but the recognizer
  yields no words yet, the voice dialog status shows "শব্দ শুনতে পাচ্ছি — গান
  মেলানো হচ্ছে…" and clears to the regular listening line on transitions.
  Result channel: RESULT_AUDIO_HINT (voice service -> activity).
- Behavior intentionally unchanged: typed search, strict voice path, pinned
  candidate rules, catalogue data, and permissions.
- Release gate status: signing secrets are NOT yet configured (CI produced
  ashke-shadek-release-unsigned-apk). Publishing a signed, update.json-backed
  release requires the permanent-key secrets plus ASHKE_STABLE_RELEASE_READY
  and a manual dispatch of the Build workflow on main.
